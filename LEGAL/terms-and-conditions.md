## 3. Description of Service

TapRush is a mobile quick-draw duel game built on the Solana blockchain. Players wager SOL against each other in 1v1 reaction-time matches. The game operates on a credit-based system:

- **Top-up**: Deposit 0.05 SOL to receive 5 play credits.
- **Matchmaking**: Join a random queue or challenge a friend via invite code.
- **Gameplay**: After a random delay, both phones vibrate. First valid tap wins.
- **Payout**: Winner receives the pot minus a 5% rake.
